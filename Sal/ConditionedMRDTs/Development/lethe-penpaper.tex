\title{\bfseries Lethe: A Core Calculus of Mergeable Forgetting\\
\large A pen-and-paper kernel for building efficient MRDTs by construction\\
(the \lean{oq:proglogic} design note)}
\author{Sal metatheory note\\ \small FP Launchpad, IIT Madras}
\date{July 9, 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Every efficient mergeable replicated data type (MRDT) in the Sal catalogue
is a \emph{forgetful image} of a free, remember-everything datatype. Lethe
makes the forgetting an operator. A term of the calculus denotes an MRDT
together with the retraction exhibiting it as an image of the free
event log; the typing derivation assembles that retraction compositionally,
and each constructor owes a fixed, leaf-level proof obligation. Four
consequences reorganize the existing framework rather than extending it:
the LCA of three-way merge is \emph{the price of forgetting}; efficiency is
its \emph{yield}; the conditioned verification-condition bundle is the
\emph{proof obligation} of the $\Forget$ operator, whose causal-delta law
$\cd$ is already machine-checked to be independent; and the observational
equivalence $\eqobs$ the framework reasons up to is the \emph{congruence}
of $\Forget$. The assertion layer is separation logic over the
\emph{id-heap}: states decompose as separating conjunctions over
identities, operations have footprints, conflict resolution is a per-atom
overlap policy, and the honesty conditions of the hardest datatype --- the
tombstone-free RGA's ancestor paths --- are list-segment assertions carried
by operations as birth certificates. No global rely appears in the
judgment.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Status.} This is a pen-and-paper design note; nothing in
it is mechanized. Every claim is either backed by an existing
kernel-checked artifact (cited inline as \lean{code}) or explicitly marked
as an obligation, a conjecture, or an open question.
\end{abstract}

\begin{remark}[Naming]
Lethe is the river of forgetting. The calculus has one non-trivial
operator, $\Forget$; the LCA is the price of forgetting; and the soundness
theorem (\S\ref{sec:aletheia}) --- the efficient term is observationally
equivalent to its free, remember-everything expansion --- is an
\emph{un-forgetting} theorem, so it is named \textbf{Aletheia}
(\emph{a-l\={e}th\={e}}: truth as un-forgetting). The LCA-recovery law of level F2
(\S\ref{sec:ladder}) is \textbf{Euno\"e}, Lethe's paired river of restored
memory.
\end{remark}

\section{Thesis}\label{sec:thesis}

\textbf{Every efficient mergeable replicated data type is a forgetful
image of a free, remember-everything datatype.} Lethe makes the forgetting
an operator. Four consequences, each of which reorganizes an existing
piece of the framework rather than adding a new one:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{The LCA is the price of forgetting.} A datatype that
  remembers everything merges by union and never needs a common ancestor.
  Three-way merge exists because the normal form has discarded information
  that the join of two branches requires; the LCA is where it is
  recovered.
\item \textbf{Efficiency is the yield of forgetting.} ``Efficient
  OR-set'', ``closed-form counter'', ``tombstone-free RGA'' are all the
  same move: a retraction onto a normal form.
\item \textbf{The conditioned VC bundle is the proof obligation of
  $\Forget$.} The causal-delta axiom $\cd$ --- machine-checked independent
  (\lean{Refutations/CD\_Not\_Derivable\_Ternary.lean}; see
  \lean{CD\_MINIMALITY\_TERNARY.md}) --- is the primitive law of level-F2
  forgetting.
\item \textbf{$\eqobs$ is the congruence of $\Forget$.} The observational
  equivalence the framework reasons up to is not an artifact; it is the
  statement that forgetting is sound.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Preliminaries}\label{sec:prelim}

This section is self-contained; no companion note is assumed.

\paragraph{Operations and events.} Fix a type $A$ of application
operations. An \emph{event} is $\Op\,A \coloneqq \mathbb{N} \times
\mathbb{N} \times A$ --- a globally unique timestamp, a replica id, and
the payload. Timestamps are unique across the execution
(\lean{distinct\_ops}).

\paragraph{MRDT signature.} An MRDT is
$D = \langle \State,\ \init,\ \dodo,\ \mergeL,\ \rc \rangle$
where $\dodo : \State \to \Op\,A \to \State$,
$\mergeL : \State \to \State \to \State \to \State$ is the three-way merge
(first argument the LCA), and
$\rc : \Op\,A \to \Op\,A \to \{\mathsf{Fst\_then\_snd},
\mathsf{Snd\_then\_fst}, \Either\}$
arbitrates non-commuting concurrent pairs. Binary merge, when it exists,
is the slice $\Merge\,a\,b = \mergeL\,\init\,a\,b$.

\paragraph{Version DAG.} Replicas evolve by local steps (1-parent commits,
apply $\dodo$) and merge steps (2-parent commits, apply $\mergeL$ at the
DAG-LCA of the parents). A \emph{configuration} is coherent when every
merge's LCA argument is the fold of the shared causal prefix of its
parents.

\begin{remark}[Load-bearing caution]\label{rem:caution}
In a general version DAG two branches may share events \emph{beyond} their
LCA (the same operation delivered to both sides via different merges), so
no per-triple state equation of the form
$\nu(A \sqcup B) = m(\nu L, \nu A, \nu B)$ can be a \emph{definition} of
correctness. This is exactly why the framework's contract is a
DAG-inductive VC bundle and why no lattice-flavoured contract can exist
(\lean{mrdt-metatheory.pdf}, delta-contract section). Lethe inherits this:
the F2 obligation (\S\ref{sec:ladder}) is stated per \emph{reachable
configuration}, not per triple.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{The judgment.} $D$ is \emph{RA-linearizable up to $\eqobs$}
when at every version of every reachable coherent configuration, the state
is $\eqobs$-equal to the fold ($\dodo$ over $\init$) of some linearization
of the version's visible events that respects causal order and $\rc$.
Here $\eqobs$ is an equivalence on $\State$, congruent for $\dodo$ and
$\mergeL$, under which queries are invariant. For datatypes whose
operations are only meaningful on some states, the judgment is conditioned
on \emph{honest delivery}: every op is applicable at each state it is
applied to, and \emph{accurate at birth} (its payload correctly describes
the issuing state). Only level F3 and $\Guard$ consume honesty; F0--F2
derivations are honesty-free.

\paragraph{The metatheorem interface.} The framework provides:
(i)~the flat ternary metatheorem ---
$\co + \fd + \cd \Rightarrow \mathsf{JoinLemma3} \Rightarrow \RA$
(eight VCs, validated, minimal at $\cd$);
(ii)~the conditioned metatheorem consuming honest delivery (the
tombstone-free RGA's route,
\lean{MRDT\_Instances/RGA/RA\_Lin.lean});
(iii)~the product kit (\lean{Metatheory/Product*.lean}): convergence by
concatenation witness, one-sided safety, and the $\eqobs$-quotient lift.
Lethe treats these as the semantic soundness lemmas its typing rules will
invoke; this note adds no new metatheory.

\section{The Free Object: the Log}\label{sec:log}

\begin{definition}[The log]\label{def:log}
\[
\Log(A):\quad
\begin{aligned}[t]
\State &\coloneqq \mathsf{Finset}\,(\Op\,A) &
\init &\coloneqq \varnothing \\
\dodo\ s\ o &\coloneqq \{o\} \cup s &
\mergeL\ l\ a\ b &\coloneqq a \cup b
  \quad\text{($l$ unused: remembering is free)} \\
\rc\ \_\ \_ &\coloneqq \Either &
\eqobs\; &\coloneqq\; (=)
\end{aligned}
\]
\end{definition}

On coherent configurations $l \subseteq a \cap b$, so union is the pushout
of the two branches over the LCA; every linearization folds to the same
union, and RA-linearizability is trivial. $\Log(A)$ is \emph{free} in the
operative sense:

\begin{quote}
For any MRDT $D$ over the same operations, RA-linearizability of $D$ says
exactly that every reachable state of $D$ is ($\eqobs$ to) the image of a
reachable $\Log$-state under $D$'s own fold. \textbf{Correct $=$ a
forgetful image of the log.}
\end{quote}

The catalogue proves this datatype-by-datatype. Lethe's job is to build
the images \emph{compositionally}: a term denotes a datatype together with
the retraction exhibiting it as an image, and the typing derivation
assembles the global retraction from per-constructor pieces.

\section{The Id-Heap and the Assertion Layer}\label{sec:idheap}

\paragraph{Id-heap.} A Lethe state is presented as a separating
conjunction of cells indexed by keys (element identities, timestamps,
replica ids, unit for singleton cells):
\[
\sigma \;\vDash\; \sepmap_{k \in K}\ \mathsf{cell}_k .
\]

\paragraph{Assertions.}
\[
P \;::=\; \emp
  \;\mid\; k \mapsto v
  \;\mid\; P * P
  \;\mid\; \lseg(k_0 \cdots k_n)
  \;\mid\; \tok(n)
  \;\mid\; \ulcorner \varphi \urcorner
\]
$k \mapsto v$ --- key $k$ holds cell value $v$; $*$ --- disjoint
composition; $\lseg(k_0 \cdots k_n)$ --- a chain
$k_0 \mapsto (\_, k_1) * \cdots * k_{n-1} \mapsto (\_, k_n)$ in a
parent-pointer cell family (the RGA ancestor path); $\tok(n)$ --- $n$
escrow tokens (the bounded-counter / budget discipline);
$\ulcorner \varphi \urcorner$ --- pure facts.

\paragraph{Footprints and locality.} Each operation has a footprint
$\fp(o) \subseteq K$ --- the keys its $\dodo$ reads or writes.

\begin{proposition}[Locality principle]\label{prop:locality}
$\fp(o_1) \cap \fp(o_2) = \varnothing$ implies $o_1, o_2$ commute on all
states, and $\rc(o_1,o_2) = \Either$ is forced.
\end{proposition}

Consequently $\rc$ is not a global relation but a \textbf{per-atom overlap
policy $\rho$}: a choice of arbitration for same-footprint concurrent
pairs (add-wins, remove-wins, larger-timestamp-wins, \dots). Composite
$\rc$ is computed pointwise; cross-component pairs are $\Either$ by frame.
This is the separation-logic reading of the repository's empirical fact
that every production $\rc$ is $\Either$ except on same-key pairs
(\lean{osRc}; the RGA's $\Either$-everywhere; the mergeable queue's
\emph{absence} of any valid $\rc$ being the flag that its operations share
one footprint --- the whole sequence).

\paragraph{Certificates.} An operation may carry an assertion over the
id-heap --- its \emph{certificate} --- required to hold at the issuing
state (birth) and to be re-establishable at delivery. F0--F2 operations
carry $\emp$. The tombstone-free RGA's $\mathsf{Ins}/\mathsf{Del}$ carry
$\lseg(\mathit{prefix} \mathbin{+\!\!+} [\mathit{anchor}])$: the recorded
ancestor path \emph{is} a list-segment assertion, and honest delivery
\emph{is} certificate validity. This is the design decision that replaces
a global rely: what the environment may do is bounded by what certificates
it must preserve, checked per footprint.

\section{The Kernel}\label{sec:kernel}

\subsection{Grammar}

\[
\begin{array}{r@{\;\;}c@{\;\;}l@{\qquad}l}
\text{atoms} & A &::= \GSet\,\tau
  & \text{grow-only set of $\tau$ (the free cell)} \\
 & &\mid\; \MaxCell\,(\tau, <)
  & \text{write-max register ($\bot$-initialized)} \\
 & &\mid\; \Agree\,\tau
  & \text{write-once cell} \\[6pt]
\text{terms} & D &::= A^{\rho} & \textsc{(Atom)} \\
 & &\mid\; D_1 \otimes D_2 & \textsc{(Prod)} \\
 & &\mid\; \sepmap_{k : K}\, D & \textsc{(Map)} \\
 & &\mid\; \Forget[\mu]_{\nu,\,\iota,\,\nuop}\, D & \textsc{(Forget)} \\
 & &\mid\; \Guard_{I,\,\app}\, D & \textsc{(Guard)}
\end{array}
\]

Here $\rho$ is an overlap policy (the atom's $\rc$ on same-footprint
pairs); $\nu : \State_D \to \NF$ is a retraction with section
$\iota : \NF \to \State_D$, $\nu \circ \iota = \mathrm{id}$; $\nuop$ is
the action of the retraction on operations (identity except at F3,
\S\ref{sec:ladder}); and $\mu \in \{\cc, \flat, \wit\}$ is the \emph{join
species}: which soundness route the owed Join Lemma takes
(conditioned-commutation, flat/unconditional, or direct linearization
witness). The species is a mode annotation, not a new judgment: the
framework already ships all three as proved routes (the flat metatheorem;
the conditioned metatheorem; the mergeable queue's direct witness
\lean{queue\_ra\_linearizable3}).

\begin{remark}[Near-single-atom basis]
In principle the atom basis is $\GSet$ alone ---
$\MaxCell = \Forget[\flat](\GSet)$ with $\nu = \max$ (an F1 forgetting)
and $\Agree$ is its degenerate write-once form. They are kept primitive as
a \emph{pre-discharged library}: their laws are proved once and
derivations cite them.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The typing judgment}

\[
D \;\vdash\; \judg{\Sigma}{\eqobs}{O}
\]

$\Sigma$ --- the denoted MRDT signature; $\eqobs$ --- the equivalence the
judgment is up to; $O$ --- the multiset of \emph{owed obligations}:
leaf-level proof debt that a derivation leaves to the datatype author.
Aletheia (\S\ref{sec:aletheia}) states: if
$D \vdash \judg{\Sigma}{\eqobs}{O}$ and every obligation in $O$ is
discharged, then $\Sigma$ is RA-linearizable up to $\eqobs$ (under honest
delivery iff an F3 $\Forget$ or a $\Guard$ occurs in $D$), and $\Sigma$
$\eqobs$-refines its free expansion.

\subsection{Rules}

\paragraph{\textsc{(Atom)}.}
\begin{mathpar}
\inferrule*[right=Atom]
  { }
  {A^{\rho} \;\vdash\; \judg{\Sigma_A \text{ with } \rc = \rho
    \text{ on overlap},\ \Either \text{ off}}{(=)}{\{\text{atom laws of } A\}}}
\end{mathpar}
The atom laws (semilattice laws for $\GSet$; totality of $<$ for
$\MaxCell$; single-writer for $\Agree$) are library facts, discharged
once.

\paragraph{\textsc{(Prod)}.}
\begin{mathpar}
\inferrule*[right=Prod]
  {D_1 \vdash \judg{\Sigma_1}{\eqobs_1}{O_1} \\
   D_2 \vdash \judg{\Sigma_2}{\eqobs_2}{O_2} \\
   K(D_1) \cap K(D_2) = \varnothing}
  {D_1 \otimes D_2 \;\vdash\;
   \judg{\Sigma_1 \times \Sigma_2 \text{ pointwise}}
        {\eqobs_1 \times \eqobs_2}{O_1 \cup O_2}}
\end{mathpar}
\textbf{No new obligation.} This is the frame rule, and it is the one
rule already fully mechanized: convergence by concatenation witness,
one-sided safety, and the $\eqobs$-quotient lift
(\lean{Metatheory/Product*.lean}; first real consumer PeritextTF, 1{,}064
lines for rich text). The side condition is syntactic: disjoint
key-spaces, hence cross $\rc = \Either$ by
Proposition~\ref{prop:locality}. The known semantic caution ---
reachability does not project through $\otimes$ --- is \emph{why} the
rule owes a concatenation witness rather than a naive pairing; the
witness is proved once, in the kit, not per instance.

\paragraph{\textsc{(Map)}.} $\sepmap_{k:K} D$ --- the keyed family:
operations address one key, state is the finitely-supported product.
Semantically an iterated $\otimes$ with uniform components; \textbf{no
new obligation} beyond $D$'s, \emph{provided} each operation's footprint
is a single key (syntactic check). Owed to the mechanization: the
pointwise lift as a once-only theorem (it exists today only implicitly,
inside each set/map datatype's own discharge).

\paragraph{\textsc{(Forget)} --- the operator the calculus exists for.}
\begin{mathpar}
\inferrule*[right=Forget]
  {D \vdash \judg{\Sigma}{\eqobs}{O} \\
   \nu : \Sigma.\State \to \NF \\
   \iota : \NF \to \Sigma.\State \\
   \nu \circ \iota = \mathrm{id}}
  {\Forget[\mu]_{\nu,\iota,\nuop}\, D \;\vdash\;
   \judg{\Sigma_\NF}{\eqobs_\nu}{O \cup O_F(\text{level},\mu)}}
\end{mathpar}
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Sigma_\NF.\init &\coloneqq \nu(\Sigma.\init), &
\Sigma_\NF.\dodo\ s\ o &\coloneqq \nu\bigl(\Sigma.\dodo\ (\iota\,s)\ (\nuop\,o)\bigr), \\
\Sigma_\NF.\mergeL\ l\ a\ b &\coloneqq
  \nu\bigl(\Sigma.\mergeL\ (\iota\,l)\ (\iota\,a)\ (\iota\,b)\bigr), &
\eqobs_\nu &\coloneqq \text{the $\nu$-kernel quotient of } \eqobs .
\end{aligned}
\]
The owed obligation $O_F$ is stratified by \emph{how much $\nu$ forgets}
--- the ladder, \S\ref{sec:ladder}. $\nuop = \mathrm{id}$ except at F3.

\paragraph{\textsc{(Guard)}.} $\Guard_{I,\app} D$ --- the conditioning
wrapper: invariant $I$ on states, applicability $\app$ on (state, op).
Denotation unchanged; the judgment gains a safety clause ($I$ holds at
every version) and the delivery assumption strengthens to honest ($\app$
at each application). Owed: \textbf{stability} --- $I$ is preserved by
$\dodo$ of applicable operations and by $\mergeL$ of $I$-states arising in
honest configurations --- discharged by one of the two proved safety
routes (causal-witness or escrow; both landed in the conditioning kits).
The escrow instance: $\app$ consumes $\tok(n)$ resources whose total is
bounded by $I$ --- the bounded counter is exactly this
(\lean{bc\_version\_inv}). \textbf{The rule must be allowed to refuse}:
see \S\ref{sec:refusals} for FWW-mutex and the ungated BudgetCart budget,
where refusal is the correct and already-established outcome.

\section{The Forgetting Ladder}\label{sec:ladder}

Stratify $\Forget$ by what the retraction destroys.

\begin{conjecture}[C1: ladder strictness]\label{conj:ladder}
Each level's obligations strictly contain the previous level's. The
inclusions are evident; strictness at each step is witnessed by the
exemplar datatypes below (see also Open
Question~\ref{oq:strictness}).
\end{conjecture}

\paragraph{F0 --- no forgetting} ($\nu = \mathrm{id}$). Grow-only
datatypes; merge is union; obligations: atom laws only.
\emph{Exemplars:} G-set, G-map, tombstoned RGA, Peritext (all components
grow-only, pointwise union).

\paragraph{F1 --- join-compatible forgetting.} The kernel of $\nu$ is a
semilattice congruence:
\begin{obligation}[F1-law]\label{obl:f1}
$\nu(x \sqcup y) \;=\; \nu\bigl(\iota(\nu\,x) \sqcup \iota(\nu\,y)\bigr)$
for all reachable $x, y$.
\end{obligation}
The normal forms then carry an induced \emph{binary} join; the datatype
remains a CRDT after forgetting, and no LCA appears. \emph{Exemplars:}
LWW register ($\nu =$ keep-max: the max of a union is the max of maxes),
FWW register (min-cell, the positive complement of the
\lean{lww\_merge\_needs\_timestamps} kill-test), plausibly MVR ($\nu =$
keep the undominated antichain --- \emph{tentative, to validate by
hand-derivation}, RQ1).

\paragraph{F2 --- LCA-recoverable forgetting.} $\nu$ is \emph{not} a
join-congruence: the join of images is not determined by the images. The
lost information is recovered from the LCA. The obligation is
\textbf{Euno\"e}, stated at the only level of generality that survives
Remark~\ref{rem:caution} (branches may share beyond the LCA):

\begin{obligation}[Euno\"e, the F2 obligation]\label{obl:eunoe}
There is a normal-form merge $m(l, a, b)$ such that in every reachable
coherent configuration, $m$ at the version-DAG's LCA-images equals the
$\nu$-image of the log-level union --- inductively over the DAG.
Concretely: the shipped VC bundle $\co + \fd + \cd$ for $\Sigma_\NF$,
under join species $\mu$.
\end{obligation}

The shape of $m$ is \emph{inclusion--exclusion}: subtract from the
branches what the LCA shows to be shared history. Three instances of the
same formula:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule
datatype & normal form & $m(l, a, b)$ \\
\midrule
counter & event count & $a + b - l$ \\
OR-set & live instances &
  $(l \cap a \cap b) \cup (a \setminus l) \cup (b \setminus l)$
  \ ($=$ \lean{osMergeL}) \\
monoid cell, general & fold of events & branch deltas re-applied to $l$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

$\cd$ is the causal-delta law of this level, and its machine-checked
independence (\lean{cdvc3\_not\_derivable\_from\_core\_delta}) is now read
as: \textbf{the Euno\"e law is a primitive axiom of the calculus, not
derivable from the core and delta laws --- the rule basis is minimal at
F2.} \emph{Exemplars:} IO-counter, PN-counter, OR-set, efficient OR-set
(same free term, two retractions --- the ``efficient'' variant is a
\emph{further} $\nu$; the calculus makes ``efficient'' a relation between
terms, not a new datatype), EW-flag (tentative), AWPQ (tentative), and the
mergeable queue at species $\wit$ (no $\rc$ exists --- the enqueue clique
shares the sequence footprint --- so $\cc$ is structurally unavailable and
the Join Lemma is the direct witness, exactly
\lean{queue\_ra\_linearizable3}).

\paragraph{F3 --- certificate-carrying forgetting.} $\nu$ destroys state
that the \emph{operations' own semantics} reads: after forgetting, an
operation's meaning is no longer a function of the normal form it is
applied to. Two things become necessary, and the calculus makes both
explicit where the framework discovered them semantically:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{$\nuop \neq \mathrm{id}$} --- the retraction acts on
  operations: payloads are extended with certificates
  (\S\ref{sec:idheap}) recording the destroyed context at birth, and
  $\dodo$ on normal forms consults the certificate.
\item \textbf{Honest delivery} --- born-valid certificates, applicable at
  delivery --- enters the judgment; it is consumed by nothing below F3.
\end{enumerate}
Obligation: Euno\"e \emph{plus} honesty-threading (born accuracy $+$
applicability, inductively over the DAG) \emph{plus} correctness of
$\nuop$ (the rewritten operation on the normal form simulates the original
operation on the free state, up to $\eqobs_\nu$). \emph{Exemplar (the only
one):} the tombstone-free RGA. Free term: the tombstoned RGA (an F0
term!). $\nu$: drop dead nodes, rehome children along recorded parent
chains. Certificates:
$\lseg(\mathit{prefix} \mathbin{+\!\!+} [\mathit{anchor}])$.
$\eqobs_\nu$: ghost payloads --- and the eq-variant result
(\lean{RGA\_Tombstone\_Free\_Eq\_MRDT.lean}: on normal forms, $\eqobs$
\emph{is} structural equality) is precisely the statement that
$\eqobs_\nu$ is the $\nu$-kernel quotient and nothing more. The
impossibility of a prefix-free variant
(\lean{RGA\_PrefixFree\_Impossible.lean}) becomes a calculus theorem-shape:
\textbf{F3 without certificates is underivable} (\S\ref{sec:refusals}).

\paragraph{Orthogonal axis --- $\Guard$.} Safety conditioning composes
with any level: BC $= \Guard_{\text{escrow}}(\text{F2 counter})$;
BudgetCart $= \Guard(\sepmap\, \text{OR-set with derived spend})$ with the
budget theorem hypothesis-gated (OQ8 --- the refusal is the framework's,
and the calculus must reproduce it, \S\ref{sec:refusals}).

\begin{remark}[The ladder retro-dicts the difficulty gradient]
F0/F1 datatypes discharged with \lean{dsimp+grind} almost entirely; F2
needed the conditioned metatheory; the single F3 datatype needed
\lean{HonestDelivery} and a bespoke end-to-end proof. The calculus did not
choose this ordering --- the proofs did.
\end{remark}

\section{Worked Derivations}\label{sec:derivations}

Component-by-component, per the house format. Certificates are $\emp$
unless stated.

\subsection{LWW register --- $\Forget[\flat]_{\text{keep-max}}\bigl(\GSet\,(\mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{N} \times \tau)\bigr)$\quad[F1]}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\toprule

\section{Aletheia: the Soundness Theorem (statement owed, not proved)}
\label{sec:aletheia}

\begin{theorem}[Aletheia]\label{thm:aletheia}
If $D \vdash \judg{\Sigma}{\eqobs}{O}$ and every obligation in $O$ is
discharged, then:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \emph{(convergence)} $\Sigma$ is RA-linearizable up to $\eqobs$ at
  every version of every reachable coherent configuration --- under honest
  delivery iff $D$ contains an F3 $\Forget$ or a $\Guard$, unconditionally
  otherwise;
\item \emph{(un-forgetting)} the composite retraction $\nu_D$ assembled
  along the derivation exhibits $\llbracket D \rrbracket$
  $\eqobs$-refining its free expansion --- every reachable $\Sigma$-state
  is $\nu_D$ of a reachable free state, and queries factor through
  $\nu_D$;
\item \emph{(safety)} every $\Guard_I$ node's $I$ holds at every version,
  given the client honesty contract.
\end{enumerate}
Proved by \emph{one induction on the typing derivation}.
\end{theorem}

Case-by-case, what exists versus what is owed:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{2.6in}p{2.1in}@{}}
\toprule
rule case & semantic lemma & status \\
\midrule
\textsc{Atom} & atom laws
  & exists piecemeal in instance discharges; owed: library form \\
\textsc{Prod} & concatenation witness $+$ safety $+$ $\eqobs$-lift
  & \textbf{exists} (\lean{Product*.lean}) \\
\textsc{Map} & pointwise lift
  & owed (implicit in per-datatype proofs today) \\
\textsc{Forget} F1 & join-congruence $\Rightarrow$ CRDT & easy; owed \\
\textsc{Forget} F2 & Euno\"e $\Rightarrow$ VC bundle $\Rightarrow$
  $\mathsf{JoinLemma3}$ $\Rightarrow$ \RA
  & \textbf{exists} (flat $+$ conditioned metatheorems); owed: the
    transport along $\nu$ as a lemma rather than per-instance \\
\textsc{Forget} F3 & honesty-threading $+$ $\nuop$ simulation
  & exists as the RGA instance (\lean{RA\_Lin.lean}); owed: rule-generic
    form \\
\textsc{Guard} & two safety routes
  & \textbf{exists} (causal-witness $+$ escrow kits) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The honest summary: \textbf{the semantic content of every case except
\textsc{Map} and the rule-generic F3 already exists kernel-checked; what
does not exist is the grammar, the composite $\nu$, and the single
induction.} That is the mechanization gap \lean{oq:proglogic} names, now
with a precise worklist.

\section{What the Calculus Predicts and Refuses}\label{sec:refusals}

A logic earns its keep by refusing derivations, and each refusal below is
\emph{already a kernel-checked or documented impossibility} --- the
calculus turns them from post-hoc discoveries into syntactic
non-derivability:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{No F3 without certificates.} A prefix-free tombstone-free
  RGA is not a Lethe term ($\nuop$ has no certificate to consult), and the
  framework proved the corresponding datatype unverifiable
  (\lean{RGA\_PrefixFree\_Impossible.lean}).
\item \textbf{No $\Guard$-mutex from merges.}
  $\Guard_{\text{unset} \Rightarrow \text{exclusive}}$ over FWW fails
  stability (``unset'' is not stable under concurrent honest extension)
  --- the documented reason the FWW register is a reservation, never a
  mutex. The \textsc{Guard} rule's stability side-condition refuses
  exactly this.
\item \textbf{No ungated BudgetCart budget.} The two-sided safety
  obligation is provably false (enumeration-dependence); \textsc{Guard}
  derives only the gated form (\lean{bcart\_version\_inv\_gated}, OQ8).
\item \textbf{No rc for the queue.} Concurrent enqueues share the
  sequence footprint; Proposition~\ref{prop:locality} makes $\rho$
  unavailable, forcing $\mu = \wit$ --- which is exactly the route the
  mechanized proof took.
\end{enumerate}

And the constructive test (\textbf{the falsifiable success criterion}):
build a datatype \emph{not in the catalogue} --- a bounded OR-set
($\Guard_{\text{escrow}}$ over \S6.3) or an LWW-map with eviction
($\sepmap_k$ over \S6.1 with a $\Forget$ dropping evicted keys) --- where
the only manual proof is the leaf Euno\"e/F1 instance. If the kernel
cannot produce something new, it is a taxonomy; if it can, it is a
calculus.

\section{Open Questions}\label{sec:oq}

\begin{openq}[OQ-L1: $\Forget$ --- one operator or two?]\label{oq:onetwo}
F3 forces an op-action $\nuop$. Is $\Forget$ a single operator with a
(usually trivial) op-action, or a state-quotient composed with an
\emph{op-compiler}? The RGA hand-derivation (\S\ref{sec:rga-derivation})
suggests the compiler view: certificates are compiled-in preconditions.
Decide on paper before mechanizing the \textsc{Forget} rule.
\end{openq}

\begin{openq}[OQ-L2: exactness of Euno\"e]\label{oq:exact}
Is the F2 obligation \emph{equivalent} to the shipped bundle
$\co + \fd + \cd$, or merely sufficient? The $\cd$ countermodel gives the
``not less'' direction; the ``not more'' direction is open. If exact, the
framework \emph{is} the soundness theorem of one operator --- the paper's
headline.
\end{openq}

\begin{openq}[OQ-L3: SL sufficiency at F3]\label{oq:sl}
Do footprint certificates $+$ per-key invariants fully replace a global
rely --- i.e., can \lean{HonestDelivery} be \emph{derived} from per-op
certificate validity, with the RGA $\lseg$ as the test? Expected yes for
F0--F2 (vacuous), genuinely open at F3.
\end{openq}

\begin{openq}[OQ-L4: efficiency as a judgment]\label{oq:eff}
$\Forget$ chains order variants of one datatype (plain vs.\ efficient
OR-set). Make ``efficient'' formal: normal-form size / merge cost versus
the free term. Even semi-formal, it justifies the word in the thesis.
\end{openq}

\begin{openq}[OQ-L5: completeness]\label{oq:complete}
Which RA-linearizable MRDTs are \emph{not} Lethe terms? The queue needed a
mode, not a new operator --- evidence the mode lattice
$\{\cc, \flat, \wit\}$ might suffice; the MVR and AWPQ hand-derivations
(\S\ref{sec:catalogue}) are the next stress tests.
\end{openq}

\begin{openq}[OQ-L6: ladder strictness --- Conjecture~\ref{conj:ladder}]
\label{oq:strictness}
Each level's obligations strictly contain the previous. Strictness
F1 $\subsetneq$ F2 is witnessed by the counter ($l$ does not drop out);
F2 $\subsetneq$ F3 by the prefix-free impossibility. F0 $\subsetneq$ F1
needs a datatype whose $\nu = \mathrm{id}$ fails F1 vacuously --- trivial,
but state it.
\end{openq}
