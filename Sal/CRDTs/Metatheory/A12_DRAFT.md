## 2. The mutual-induction attack: where it is provably circular

Architecture: absorb (CD) into `join_lemma_of_cd`'s strong induction —
at size `n = |U|`, `M(<n)` (Join Lemma below `n`) is available, so
derive CD(U,e) from `M(<n)` + axioms. Via `M(<n)`,
`A = σ(U∖e) = ⊔_{x∈U∖e} σ(↓x)` and `lem_0op` splits
`update A e = ⊔ₓ update σ(↓x) e`; equivalently, induct on
backward-closed `V` with `↓e∖e ⊆ V ⊆ U∖e` on the goal
`G(V): σ(V) ⊔ update σ(↓e∖e) e = update σ(V) e`
(base `V = ↓e∖e` is *inflation*). Peel a `loOn(V)`-maximal `x ∈
V∖(↓e∖e)`; then:

* **`x` commutes with all of `↓e ∪ {e}`**: discharge by
  `merge_peel_comm` + `commutes(e,x)` + IH `G(V∖x)`. ✓
* **`¬commutes(e,x)`, `x vis e`**: excluded (`x` would be in `↓e∖e`). ✓
* **`¬commutes(e,x)`, `e ∥ x`, `rc e x = Fst`**: impossible for a
  `loOn(V)`-maximal `x` — the forced absorber `z` of `x` would be a
  `loOn`-edge out of `x` inside `V`, or `z = e` (excluded). ✓
* **`e ∥ x`, `rc x e = Fst`** (`x` linearizes before `e`): *the wall*.
  No axiom mentions the pair (`x`-then-`e`, `rc x e = Fst`) when `e`
  has no absorber — `cond_comm_lift` constrains an rc-pair only via a
  later non-commuting witness of the *second* element, and `e` is
  maximal. Unwinding with `lem_0op` reproduces `G(V)` verbatim.
* **`commutes(e,x)` but `¬commutes(x,y)` for some `y ∈ ↓e∖e`**: the
  peel step needs `merge (update σ(V∖x) x) T = update (merge σ(V∖x)
  T) x` (`T = σ(↓e)`). One direction is free (lem_0op + absorption +
  inflation); the other reduces by `lem_0op` + `commutes(e,x)` + IH
  *exactly back to `G(V)`*. Circular. (En route, a reusable fact: `x`
  here is automatically `loOn(V ∪ ↓e)`-maximal — every `y ∈ ↓e∖{e}`
  carries an absorber inside `↓e` — its chain's first step — so
  rc-edges into a punctured downset are dead in every `loOn(S)` with
  `↓e ⊆ S`.)

So the mutual induction closes on all but two case shapes, and on
those two it is a fixed point, not a descent. **No rearrangement of
the current axioms closes (b″) by this route**; a genuinely new
principle about the pair `(rc x e = Fst, e absorber-free)` — i.e.
about deltas of maximal events over rc-predecessors — is required.

## 3. The countermodel attack: the forcing dichotomy

Target semantics for a separator: `e`'s delta must *read* state
written by some `y ∥ e` and produce content not generated by
`A ⊔ update B e`. Session attempts (numbering continues A10's six):

| # | Model | Death |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | `rem` X-delta = tagged copy `{t_e}×(A∪D)` (A10, recalled) | `cond_comm_lift`: the rc-swap difference `{(t_e,t')}` is never absorbed — tags of later events differ |
| 8 | `add` X-delta = copy of `dead` (`AWSetD`) | `cond_comm_lift` with `e''` = rem: rem can crush the add's base effect but not its X-side-effect; `X` inflationary ⇒ difference permanent |
| 9 | `rem` X-delta = untagged `A ∪ D` (`AWSetXX`) | **survives all 10 VCs** — but (CD) *holds*: the very self-push forced by `cond_comm_lift` yields the invariant `D ⊆ X` on all fold/merge states, which is exactly the accumulation (CD) needs |
| 10 | writer/sniffer (`w: W+=t`; `s: S+=t, J += {t_s}×W`), `rc w s = Fst` | `cond_comm_lift` with `e'' = w` fails (w cannot erase `(t_s,t_w)`); repairing by letting `w` pre-push `S×W`-images makes `(w,s)` *commute* — contradicting `rc_non_comm_directional`; dropping the rc lands in the commuting class, where `merge_peel_comm` directly forbids sniffing (`e`'s delta may not grow under merged-in commuting context); three-kind escapes (`w,s,k`) die on `no_rc_chain` — the `rc`-Fst digraph on op kinds must be 2-path-free, forcing the killer to commute with the writer, which re-forces pre-pushing |

**The dichotomy.** For `e`'s delta to read `y ∥ e`'s writes: either
`(y,e)` do not commute — then `rc` must order them
(`rc_non_comm_directional`), and `cond_comm_lift` + inflationarity
force every writer to pre-push the read's image (tagged images force
an infinite—but representable—push; recorded-tag finite pushes make
the pair commute), creating the accumulation invariant that
*validates* (CD); or they commute — and `merge_peel_comm` is
literally presence-insensitivity of `e`'s delta for commuting
context. `no_rc_chain` eliminates third-kind mediators. In every
constructible powerset case, the axioms transport the read content
into `A` before `e` fires.

**The caveat (where a countermodel could still live).** All these
forcing arguments decompose deltas over *atoms* (`lem_0op` makes
update-deltas join-homomorphisms on synchronized pairs, and on
powerset components join-homs are unions of element-images). On a
**non-atomic or non-distributive lattice component** the
decomposition fails and the forcing arguments do not run. The A8
fold-pair forcing (rem-crush/uncrush walks) may or may not
generalize. This is the sharpest remaining habitat.
